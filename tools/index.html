Deal & fund tools

Interactive calculators for the unglamorous math behind private markets — liquidation stacks, distribution waterfalls, and portfolio risk. Built by Vestorium; everything computes locally in your browser.

The toolkit

THREE TOOLS + ONE MODEL
01 Cap Table & SHA Simulator Pick your rights package, define the next round — up, flat, down, or structured — and see your pro-forma stake, anti-dilution shares, rights survival against covenants, and the exit waterfall. 02 Fund Operations Model European and American distribution waterfalls, management fee step-downs, capital calls, DPI / RVPI / TVPI and net IRR — live in the browser, with a full Excel model to download. 03 Portfolio Monitoring Dashboard Paste quarterly portfolio data and get covenant flags, valuation bridges, sector / vintage / geography exposure, and a macro-linked risk-sizing matrix. 04 Excel Model Download The full interlinked workbook behind Tool 02 — assumptions, portfolio, fees, both waterfalls with clawback test, and performance metrics. Formula-driven; every blue cell is yours to change.

These tools are analytical templates for education and workflow support. Figures are illustrative; nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice. Enforceability of shareholder rights depends on documentation and jurisdiction — take final documents to counsel.
